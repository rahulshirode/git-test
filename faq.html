Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before we start.

Clear process • Thoughtful work
What kind of websites do you build?

I build WordPress websites for businesses and individuals who want a site that looks good, works well, and is easy to manage after launch. That includes business websites, brochure sites, custom WordPress builds, and WooCommerce storefronts.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. I can refresh an older site, improve the layout, streamline the content, tighten up the mobile experience, fix functionality issues, and help make the site feel more aligned with how you want to present your business.

Do you work with WooCommerce?

Absolutely. I build and refine WooCommerce stores with a focus on clarity, usability, and conversion. The goal is always to make it easy for people to browse, understand, and purchase without friction.

How long does a project usually take?

It depends on the scope. A smaller site may take a few weeks, while a more extensive build or redesign will naturally take longer. I’ll give you a realistic timeline after we talk through the project goals and content needs.

How do you price a website project?

Pricing is based on the project scope, complexity, and the amount of design, development, and support involved. I prefer to keep it transparent and practical, so we can plan around what you need without unnecessary complexity.

Do you help with content or design direction?

Yes. I can help shape the structure and messaging of the site, suggest practical design direction, and support the presentation of your content so it feels clear and intentional on the page.

Will I be able to manage my site after launch?

Yes. I build with simplicity in mind, so the site is easy to manage afterward. I can also provide guidance, maintenance support, or future updates depending on what you need.

What do you need from me to get started?

Usually, I need a basic understanding of your goals, any examples you like, your content, and a sense of your audience. From there, I’ll help shape the plan and guide the next steps.
